What Makes a Good Free Synth?

Not all free plugins are created equal. A good free synth should be stable in REAPER, sound high quality, include useful presets, be easy to install, and not overload your CPU. The plugins below tick all those boxes.

1. Vital – The Best Free Synth Overall

Wavetable synthesizer Beginner to advanced

Vital is, without question, the best free synth plugin available today. It's often compared to expensive synths like Serum – and in many ways it's just as powerful.

Why Vital is amazing: modern, professional sound; huge preset library; beautiful visual interface; works flawlessly in REAPER; perfect for EDM, pop, film music, and more.

If you only install ONE free synth, make it Vital.

2. Surge XT – Powerhouse All-Rounder

Multi-engine synthesizer Intermediate

Surge XT is an open-source synth that feels like a complete professional instrument, with multiple synthesis types, great built-in effects, tons of presets, and deep modulation options. This is a fantastic "do everything" synth for REAPER users who want more depth.

3. Dexed – Classic FM Synthesis

FM synthesizer Intermediate

If you love 80s sounds, electric pianos, and classic digital tones, Dexed is a must-have. It's based on the legendary Yamaha DX7 — great for retro sounds, bells and electric pianos, and bright digital leads. And it's completely free.

4. Helm – Simple and Powerful

Subtractive synthesizer Beginner friendly

Helm is a great synth for learning sound design: easy to understand, lightweight, great for basses and leads, and perfect for beginners. If Vital feels overwhelming, start with Helm.

5. TAL-Noisemaker – Classic Virtual Analog

Analog-style synth Easy

TAL-Noisemaker is simple, fun, and sounds great — perfect for pads, leads, and simple electronic sounds. It's not fancy, but it gets the job done.
