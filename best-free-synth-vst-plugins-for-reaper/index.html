What Makes a Good Free Synth?

Not all free plugins are created equal.

A good free synth should:

  • Be stable in REAPER
  • Sound high quality
  • Include useful presets
  • Be easy to install
  • Not overload your CPU

The plugins below tick all those boxes.

1. Vital – The Best Free Synth Overall

Type: Wavetable synthesizer
Difficulty: Beginner to advanced

Vital is, without question, the best free synth plugin available today.

It's often compared to expensive synths like Serum – and in many ways it's just as powerful.

Why Vital is Amazing

  • Modern, professional sound
  • Huge preset library
  • Beautiful visual interface
  • Works flawlessly in REAPER
  • Perfect for EDM, pop, film music, and more

If you only install ONE free synth, make it Vital.

2. Surge XT – Powerhouse All-Rounder

Type: Multi-engine synthesizer
Difficulty: Intermediate

Surge XT is an open-source synth that feels like a complete professional instrument.

  • Multiple synthesis types
  • Great built-in effects
  • Tons of presets
  • Deep modulation options

This is a fantastic "do everything" synth for REAPER users who want more depth.

3. Dexed – Classic FM Synthesis

Type: FM synthesizer
Difficulty: Intermediate

If you love 80s sounds, electric pianos, and classic digital tones, Dexed is a must-have.

It's based on the legendary Yamaha DX7.

Great for:

  • Retro sounds
  • Bells and electric pianos
  • Bright digital leads

And it's completely free.

4. Helm – Simple and Powerful

Type: Subtractive synthesizer
Difficulty: Beginner friendly

Helm is a great synth for learning sound design.

  • Easy to understand
  • Lightweight
  • Great for basses and leads
  • Perfect for beginners

If Vital feels overwhelming, start with Helm.

5. TAL-Noisemaker – Classic Virtual Analog

Type: Analog-style synth
Difficulty: Easy

TAL-Noisemaker is simple, fun, and sounds great.

Perfect for:

  • Pads
  • Leads
  • Simple electronic sounds

It's not fancy – but it gets the job done.
