Music Production Deals & Recommended Stores

The places I send musicians when they are ready to go beyond free tools: plugins, sample packs, DAW software and DJ music.

Free plugins and a free DAW will take you a long way. When you want more sounds, a faster workflow or a specific tool, these stores are good places to start. Most of them run regular sales, bundles and free downloads.

Plugins and software instruments

Plugin Boutique

A large plugin store with synths, effects, virtual instruments and bundles from most major developers. It often includes a free plugin with each purchase, and it runs frequent sales.

Browse Plugin Boutique

MAGIX

Maker of Music Maker, Samplitude, Sound Forge and ACID. Music Maker is a beginner-friendly loop-based DAW, and Sound Forge is a solid audio editor for cleaning up and mastering recordings.

See MAGIX software

Samples, loops and sound packs

Loopcloud

A sample library you browse from inside your DAW. Loops are matched to your project’s key and tempo, so you can audition drums, basslines and textures in context before you use them.

Try Loopcloud

Loopmasters

Royalty-free sample packs for almost every genre: drums, vocals, synths, guitar, cinematic and sound effects. A good source of free sample packs as well as paid ones.

Browse Loopmasters

Music for DJs and producers

Beatport

The main store for electronic music. Buy high-quality tracks for DJ sets, or study the charts to hear how current releases in your genre are arranged and mixed.

Visit Beatport

DJcity

A record pool for working DJs, with clean and explicit edits, intros and remixes across hip-hop, pop, dance and Latin music, all on one subscription.

Visit DJcity
